## **Learnings from LST and SST**

The internship also included PAWR and LST sessions that made the learning experience more enriching.
LST sessions were conducted every alternate Sunday by industry professionals, focusing on self-development, professional growth, communication, and corporate life.
They gave us valuable insights into what to expect when entering the professional world.
PAWR sessions, which were held for 3 weeks, focused on the latest AI trends, tools, prompting techniques, and practical applications of AI.
These sessions helped us stay updated with industry developments and encouraged continuous learning.
Overall, they provided a great balance of technical knowledge, industry exposure, and personal development.
